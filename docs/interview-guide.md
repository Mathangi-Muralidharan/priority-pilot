# Interview Guide: Round 2

**Goal:** Test whether the alignment problem from interview 1 holds across different kinds of PMs, and check hypotheses H1–H4 in [user-research.md](user-research.md).

**Who to talk to (aim for 4–6 people):**
- 1–2 PMs at large tech companies (tests H1)
- 1–2 PMs at B2B SaaS companies (tests H2)
- 1 PM at an early-stage startup (tests H3)
- 1 technical or AI/ML PM (tests H4)
- Bonus: 1 engineer or salesperson, for the stakeholder side of the story

**Ground rules**
- Ask about real past events ("Tell me about the last time…"), not hypotheticals ("Would you use…?").
- Don't mention alignment, tools, or any hypothesis first. Let them raise it. Unprompted mentions are the strongest evidence.
- Follow up with "Why?" or "Can you give an example?" instead of adding more questions.
- Record answers word for word, and note which ones were prompted.

---

## Part 1: Their world
*Same as interview 1, so answers can be compared.*

1. How do you decide which features to build next?
2. Which part of that is hardest or takes the longest?
3. What tools do you use for it today, and what's annoying about them?

## Part 2: Dig deeper
*Pick the questions that fit what they said in Part 1.*

4. Tell me about the last time a prioritization decision got pushed back on. Who pushed back, and what happened?
5. When you share priorities with stakeholders, what questions do they usually ask?
6. When two options both have a strong case, how do you break the tie?
7. Has a request from sales, leadership, or a big customer jumped the queue? How did you handle it? *(H2)*
8. How do you tell whether a request is one customer's need or a broad one? *(H2)*
9. When priorities change, what do you have to update, and who do you tell? *(H1)*
10. How do you decide how much fits into a cycle? *(H3)*
11. How do technical unknowns, like feasibility or cost, factor into the decision? *(H4)*

## Part 3: React to the product
*Send the live link: https://mathangi-muralidharan.github.io/priority-pilot/*

12. Spend 3–5 minutes with it. What's the first thing you'd use?
13. What's confusing or missing?
14. Would this make it easier to explain your priorities than your current tool? (1–5, and why?)
15. Which feature would you remove?

## Part 4: Wrap up

16. Can I quote you anonymously in a portfolio project? No confidential company, customer, or product information will be included.

## LinkedIn message to start

> Hi [Name], I'm building a feature prioritization tool as a product management portfolio project, and I'd love 10 minutes of your perspective. A few quick questions:
> 1. How do you decide which features to build next?
> 2. Which part of that is hardest or takes the longest?
> 3. What tools do you use for it today, and what's annoying about them?
>
> Happy to share what I learn from everyone. Thanks so much!

Send Parts 2–4 after they reply, so it feels like a conversation rather than a survey.
